# PLEXUS

> **The operating layer for Africa's invisible economy.**
> Run your business. Win contracts together. Build a financial identity that travels.

*PLEXUS merges two projects: NODAL (a working SME business operating system) and NEXUS
(a demand-matching network built on a verified Business Passport).*

---

## The problem

Africa's small businesses are the majority of its economy, and they are invisible three times over.

**Invisible to finance.** A trader can run fifty successful order cycles and still walk into a bank
with the credit file of someone who has never worked. No records, no risk assessment, no capital.

**Invisible to each other.** A corporate needs 5,000 branded units. Four small manufacturers could
fill it together — 1,400, 1,300, 1,200, 1,100 — but none can bid alone, and none of them knows the
other three exist. The order goes to an importer.

**Invisible to markets.** Customers two streets away buy from the chain store, because they never
knew you existed.

This isn't a lack of ambition. It's a lack of shared infrastructure.

---

## Why the gap persists

Three categories already exist, and each builds half of what's needed:

| What exists | What it does | Where it stops |
|---|---|---|
| **Campus & SME programmes** | Identify, coach and showcase entrepreneurs | Seasonal, opt-in and celebratory — a calendar, not an operating system |
| **Mobile-money credit scoring** | Builds a credit profile from transaction behaviour | Scores generic behaviour, not verified delivery of a specific contract |
| **Gig & freelance marketplaces** | Match a buyer to a seller | One transaction at a time. No aggregation, and no record that compounds |

No platform converts everyday verified delivery of real work into a portable financial record.
That's the gap.

## The solution — Run. Connect. Prove.

One operating layer for every small business: township shop, campus venture, workshop, supplier.
Each layer feeds the next.

### 1 · RUN — operate the business  `LIVE TODAY`

The business OS: point of sale and invoicing, live inventory, cash flow, suppliers, a public
storefront, and an AI coach that reads your live numbers. Priced in **Rand**, **WhatsApp-first**,
**offline-tolerant** and **voice-operable** — anyone who can talk can run it.

Every transaction becomes structured data. This is the substrate everything else is built on.

### 2 · CONNECT — find and fill demand

- **Demand ↔ capacity matching** — buyers post real orders, matched against live verified capacity
  rather than self-reported profiles.
- **Capacity aggregation** — when no single business can fill an order, PLEXUS combines several into
  one contract with one invoice, split by verified capacity and paid out directly. *This is the
  mechanic that unlocks tenders, corporate procurement and supplier-development targets.*
- **AI team assembly** — when demand exists and nobody can supply it, PLEXUS proposes a founding
  team from skills already on the network. Existing platforms match supply to demand; this creates
  supply where none exists.

### 3 · PROVE — turn work into identity

Every fulfilled contract is signed off **by the buyer**, not typed in by the seller, and written to
a portable **Business Passport**: contracts fulfilled, on-time rate, value delivered, repeat buyers.

**This is the fusion.** The record is *deep* because you run your business on PLEXUS, and *trusted*
because your buyers verified it. Neither half is worth much alone.

---

## What it unlocks

**Access to finance.** Operational data plus verified fulfilment produces a live credit score out of
850 — and the heaviest factor is the one only PLEXUS can see: work you verifiably delivered. That
score unlocks pre-qualified micro-loans, inventory BNPL and working capital. A proven Passport can
also be priced as a micro revenue-share stake — capital raised against delivery data instead of
collateral, with no debt and no equity lost.

**Data analytics.** Ranked recommendations with projected Rand impact, peer benchmarking across six
metrics, anomaly and fraud detection, and seven-day demand forecasting. Benchmarks get sharper as
the network grows, because every business is scored against real peers.

**Market connectivity.** Three doors instead of one: your own shareable storefront with WhatsApp
orders, demand matched to you without marketing spend, and contracts that were structurally out of
reach until aggregation made them fulfillable.

## How it's built

React 19 · TypeScript · Firebase Firestore (Africa-hosted) · Express AI proxy · Google Gemini with a
local Ollama fallback · Web Speech (`en-ZA`) for voice.

Production engineering, not a demo shell: **offline-first** writes that survive a total network drop,
**atomic stock transactions** so concurrent sales can never oversell, **default-deny security rules**
where the storefront is the only publicly readable path, and **graceful AI degradation** so nothing
breaks in front of a customer.

The Run layer ships today. Connect and Prove build on the same data model — no new infrastructure
required.

## Where this goes

Every business that joins makes the network worth more. Each extension below reuses the same
verified record in a new context, with no new data source:

- **Crisis response** — the supply map already exists, so 10,000 relief meals is an aggregation
  query. Hours, not days.
- **Regional trade** — each region develops a visible specialism, and the network tracks the flow
  between them.
- **Cross-border** — a proven Passport replaces the trade paperwork a small business could never
  produce.

For SMEs: capital, clarity and contracts. For buyers: verified supply at scale. For lenders:
priceable risk on businesses they previously couldn't see at all.
